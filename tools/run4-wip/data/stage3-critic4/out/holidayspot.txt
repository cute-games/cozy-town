- Easter Bunny stands right in front of the pink blossom tree. When you walk up to talk, the treetop sits on its head like a big pink hat. Move the bunny a step to the side.
- The cat costume in the mirror looks like grey pyjamas: no ears, tail or whiskers. Its "it's yours!" bubble also covers the kid's chin.
- Christmas tree: the very dark red baubles look like holes, and the top is a plain gold cone. Use bright baubles and a real star.
- Santa shows no coins until you talk to him. A coin bag or a gold coin in his waving hand would show he gives coins.
- There is no October night picture and no fireworks picture, so the purple path, lamps, spooky signs, ghosts, pumpkins and sparks are not checked.
