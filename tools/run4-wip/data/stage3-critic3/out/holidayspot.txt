SCORE: 7
The school door's pale lintel pokes through the red "School party today!" banner and hides the top of "party" – lift the banner or push it forward so the words are whole.
Up close Santa has his back to the player, even while he speaks (we see his hat band and neck, no face) – turn him (and the bunny) to face the player when talked to; his smiling face from the gate is lovely.
Confetti on 31 Dec is a handful of tiny specks – more, bigger, brighter pieces drifting down so it reads as a party.
The flat yellow/grey "birds" over the Easter corner and the flat blue one lying on the grass by the bunny look like floating planks and litter – give them a small body or drop them.
No October picture was given, so the purple path, lamps, ghosts, pumpkins and the two spooky signs could not be checked; the gift boxes under the tree are plain cubes and a ribbon cross would make them read as presents.
