# Dance-off choreography

Each Funky phrase occupies one or two beats on the song clock. The same chart assigns arrow variants to both dancers and to spectators. The first eight phrases are solo adaptations for the Cozy Town rig, which has shoulder and hip joints. The arm sweep suggests the wrist roll, and the bent silhouette of the Funky Chicken comes from the shoulders.

| Trigger | Phrase | Beats | Movement |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Left | Saturday Night Fever disco point | 2 | Settle onto one foot, tip the hip, look up toward the high finger, then point across. |
| Left | Hustle rock-and-turn | 2 | Rock back, recover forward, and swivel the supporting foot and shoulders. |
| Down | The Bump | 1 | Sit into the supporting leg, then bump the hip out as the opposite arm counters it. |
| Down | Funky Chicken | 2 | Give two outward shoulder pumps, nod, and alternate foot taps. |
| Up | Locking point and wrist roll | 1 | Circle the lead arm, shift the weight, and stop sharply in a point. |
| Up | Bus Stop grapevine | 2 | Side step, cross behind, side step, and tap in. |
| Right | Kick-ball-change | 1 | Flick forward, put the ball of the foot down, and switch weight. |
| Right | Robot arm wave | 2 | Pass the wave from one arm through the shoulders to the other with small head and leg isolations. |
| 5 combo | Scoo B Doo | 2 | Kick, switch the feet, and hit a two-arm lock. |
| 10 combo | Hustle spot turn into a disco point | 2 | Turn on the spot and finish facing front in a high point. |

Between moves, both shoes stay planted in a small knee bounce.

## Ideas for the other song types

| Type | Dance styles | Six moves to try |
|---|---|---|
| Sweet | Social waltz, swing and cha-cha | Waltz box step, waltz twinkle, underarm turn, side chassé, swing rock step, cha-cha New Yorker |
| Pixel | Popping, robot and tutting | Robot walk, ticking, dime stop, box tut, forearm wave, heel-toe |
| Mystery | Animation, gliding and popping | Slow-motion walk, backslide, body wave, chest pop, puppet, scarecrow |
| Fast | House and shuffle | Running Man, T-step, Charleston, kick-ball-change, shuffle kick, heel-toe swivel |

Sources for the other styles. [Stanford Social Dance syllabi](https://socialdance.stanford.edu/syllabi.htm), [STEEZY popping foundations](https://www.steezy.co/posts/how-to-learn-popping), [STEEZY Running Man](https://www.steezy.co/posts/how-to-do-the-running-man).
